Classic Ludo rules

All four pawns start in the yard. Roll 6 to enter a pawn on your colored start square, or move a pawn already in play by six. Entering consumes the roll.

Move around the 52-square outer track, then through your five-square home lane. An exact roll is required to finish. The eight ★ squares are safe: track positions 0, 8, 13, 21, 26, 34, 39 and 47. Pawns cannot be captured there.

Friendly pawns may share and pass through a square; they never form a blockade. A 6, capture, or finish gives one extra roll. A third consecutive 6 is cancelled and ends the turn. First player to finish all four pawns wins. Friendly pawns may share and pass through one another; they do not form blockades.

Color Cards rules

The 108-card deck has red, yellow, green and blue number/action cards plus Wild and Wild Draw Four. Seven cards are dealt each. Setup always chooses a numeric opening discard; rejected action/Wild cards are returned and reshuffled.

Match color, number or action, or play a Wild. Draw exactly one card; only that newly drawn card may then be played. Draw Two and Wild Draw Four never stack. Wild Draw Four may be challenged and is legal only when the player held no card matching the active color.

With one card left, press UNO! when playing. If it was not declared, an opponent may Catch before the next player begins an action; a valid catch draws two. Single-round and 500-point matches are supported.

Offline play works after the app has been loaded and cached successfully once; the first visit requires an internet connection. Online turns use a 45-second server timer and a 60-second reconnection grace period. A disconnected online match is never converted into an offline match.

Offline: the first visit needs an internet connection so the service worker can cache the app and local assets. After that successful load, offline Ludo and Color Cards can run without a network.
