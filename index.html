UNOby DaniLabs

Match color or number. Draw one when you cannot play. First to empty their hand wins.

Core UNO rules: ⊘ skips, ⇄ reverses, +2 draws two, ★ changes color, and +4 changes color and draws four. Draw cards do not stack.

UNOby DaniLabs

How to play

Roll a 6 to bring a token out. Move tokens clockwise around the board and up your home column. You need the exact roll to reach the center.

Land on an opponent (not on a ★ safe cell) to send them home. Two of your tokens on one cell are a block and cannot be captured (you can turn this off in Settings). A 6, a capture, or reaching the center gives another turn. Three 6s in a row lose the turn.

Glowing tokens with a ▲ can move. Tap one to move it. First to finish all four tokens wins.

Ludo styles: Classic, Quick (2 tokens each) and Rush (all tokens start on the track). There is also a separate card game on the home screen.

UNO is available from Home for offline play, and from Online Play for private online rooms.
